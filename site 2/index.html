About

Self‑Funded & Self‑Taught

100%self-funded from my own businesses
Every tradelogged with a date, price and proof
Every positionbacked by a written thesis and sell triggers

I'm Thomas. Every dollar in this portfolio is money I earned myself, most of it from summers running my own businesses: Bronte Window Cleaning and T-Tops Detailing, a mobile car-detailing service. None of it was inherited or handed to me, so every position here matters to me deeply.

I'm teaching myself to invest and documenting it as I go. Each holding comes with a written report: why I bought it, how big the position is, what I think the company can become, and what would make me sell. The trade log and broker statements are here so anyone can check the record, including the mistakes.

The goal is to sharpen my judgment and build my own system for why I buy what I buy, how much risk I take, and what I expect from every company I own.
